Incoterms 2020

11 Incoterms (2020 Edition)Defined by the International Chamber of Commerce (ICC). Split into 7 terms for any transport mode (EXW, FCA, CPT, CIP, DAP, DPU, DDP) and 4 sea-only terms (FAS, FOB, CFR, CIF). They define who pays and who bears risk.

6

Insurance Clauses

6 Insurance Clause TypesICC (A) All Risks, ICC (B) Named Perils, ICC (C) Major Perils for marine transit. ITC (A), ITC (B), ITC (C) for inland transit. Coverage ranges from comprehensive to basic depending on the clause chosen.
